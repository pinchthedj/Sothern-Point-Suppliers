About Us

Southern Point Suppliers (Pty) Ltd is a South African Information and Communication Technology (ICT) supply company based in Pretoria, Gauteng. The company specializes in the procurement, supply, and delivery of ICT hardware, software, networking equipment, peripherals, and related technology solutions to private sector companies, public entities, and government departments.

Southern Point Suppliers operates through strategic partnerships with leading ICT distributors in South Africa, enabling the company to offer a wide range of reputable products from globally recognized manufacturers and software publishers.

Our Mission

To provide quality ICT products and software solutions through strategic partnerships with leading distributors and vendors while maintaining professionalism, integrity, and operational excellence.

Our Vision

To become a trusted and preferred ICT procurement partner for businesses and government institutions across South Africa by delivering reliable technology solutions, competitive pricing, and exceptional customer service.
